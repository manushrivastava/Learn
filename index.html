About Me

I am a Computer Science academic and researcher with over 15 years of teaching and research experience, and a Ph.D. in Engineering from Kyushu Institute of Technology (Kyutech), Japan, supported by Japan's prestigious MEXT Scholarship. My research centers on Natural Language Processing and Neural Machine Translation — particularly ontology population — alongside a growing focus on Quantum Computing and Deep Neural Networks. I currently serve as Assistant Professor at Manipal University Jaipur, where I teach, mentor students, and contribute to departmental research and academic governance.
